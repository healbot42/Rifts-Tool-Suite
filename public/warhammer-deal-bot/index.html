Warhammer Deal Bot

Watch miniature listings and receive email alerts when they match your chosen price and condition rules.

This personal-use Python application runs on your own Windows computer. It searches eBay through the official Browse API, compares listings with your configured thresholds, and keeps local price and alert history. It does not place orders or purchase items.

Email alerts with your permission

You can connect your Gmail account through Google's sign-in page. The bot requests only permission to send email on your behalf, using gmail.send, so it can deliver deal digests and explicit test messages to the recipient you configure. That permission does not allow reading or deleting your inbox.

This website provides information about the bot. Google sign-in and monitoring are launched from the locally installed application; they do not run on this website.

What you control

  • Your watchlist, acceptable conditions, price thresholds, and email recipient.
  • Whether email alerts are enabled and when the bot runs.
  • When to disconnect Google access or remove local history.

eBay is the currently implemented live listing source. Other retailer and Reddit integrations remain disabled. Live searches require your own approved eBay API access.

Privacy and support

Read the privacy policy for data access, storage, sharing, retention, and deletion details, and the terms of use before using the bot.

Warhammer Deal Bot follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, when using information received from Google APIs.

For support or privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the repository's issue tracker. Issues are public; do not include credentials, tokens, or private email content.
