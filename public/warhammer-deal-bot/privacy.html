Privacy policy

Effective September 8, 2026. This policy describes the Warhammer Deal Bot application maintained by healbot42 and these informational pages.

Purpose and data used

The bot runs on your computer to search configured miniature listings and optionally send deal notifications. It uses your watchlist, price rules, listing details, configured sender and recipient addresses, and generated alert content. Listing details can include seller information supplied by eBay.

Google account access

When you choose Gmail OAuth, Google handles sign-in and consent. The bot requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. It uses this permission to send deal alerts and test messages through your account to the configured recipient. It does not request access to read inbox messages, contacts, calendars, or files, or to delete email. The bot does not receive your Google account password.

The application receives OAuth access and refresh tokens and uses your downloaded desktop OAuth client configuration to maintain your authorization. Gmail delivery responses are used to determine whether sending succeeded; they are not saved as mailbox history.

Storage and protection

The Windows OAuth implementation saves the refresh token and OAuth client configuration in Windows Credential Manager under the Windows account that authorized the bot. Short-lived access tokens are used in memory and are not saved to a token file. The downloaded client JSON remains wherever you saved it. Software running as your Windows user may be able to access that user's credential vault.

Your product settings and sender/recipient configuration remain on your computer. A local SQLite database stores listing observations, prices, source-run records, and alert history. Operational logs may contain timestamps, counts, and errors. Gmail authorization errors are sanitized, and authentication debug logging is suppressed to avoid exposing tokens.

If you explicitly choose legacy SMTP instead of OAuth, you supply an SMTP username and app password through local environment variables. This option does not use the OAuth credential vault.

Data sharing and third-party services

The bot sends OAuth requests to Google and sends your message content, sender, and recipient information to Gmail for delivery. The recipient and their email provider receive the resulting email. It sends your configured search queries and eBay API credentials or access tokens to eBay as needed for listing searches. Google and eBay also receive normal network connection information when contacted.

The application has no developer-operated backend that collects your account data or local database. It does not sell personal data, use Google data for advertising, or use Google data to train AI models. It does not upload Gmail tokens, configuration, or local history to this website or the public source repository.

Google, eBay, and recipient email providers process the data they receive under their own policies. These informational pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process visitor connection information under the GitHub privacy statement. These pages add no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies, or browser storage.

Google Limited Use

Warhammer Deal Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the email notification functionality described here.

Retention, disconnection, and deletion

Local authorization is retained until you remove it; Google may separately expire or revoke it. Run python -m warhammer_deal_bot gmail-forget as the Windows user who connected Gmail to remove the bot's saved grant from Credential Manager. Revoke the app in Google account connections to terminate its Google access as well.

Local listing and alert history has no automatic deletion period. To remove it, stop the bot and its scheduled task, then delete its configured SQLite database. Remove your local configuration, downloaded OAuth client JSON, and any bot logs or backups if you no longer need them. Remove bot-specific environment variables to clear locally configured addresses and API credentials.

Disconnecting the bot or deleting its database does not delete messages already sent. Manage those copies through Gmail and the recipient's mailbox. The maintainer cannot delete data stored only on your computer or in your mailbox.

Contact and changes

For questions, contact healbot42 through the repository's issue tracker. Use general descriptions because issues are public. Do not post passwords, tokens, or private messages. Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
